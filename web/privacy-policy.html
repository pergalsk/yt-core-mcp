Privacy Policy — yt-core-mcp

Last updated: 2026-11-22

yt-core-mcp ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on the user's own computer. It is an MCP server that lets an AI assistant manage the user's own YouTube channel (live stream scheduling, video metadata, thumbnails) through the official YouTube APIs.

Who runs the app

The app is developed and used by Peter Gál. Contact: pergalsk@gmail.com

What data the app accesses

After you sign in with your Google account and agree, the app can access:

Permission (OAuth scope) Why
youtube Manage live broadcasts, streams, videos, thumbnails and playlists on your channel
youtube.force-ssl (optional) Manage captions and comments
yt-analytics.readonly (optional) Read channel analytics

Optional permissions are only requested when you add them yourself.

How data is used

  • YouTube data is used only to do what you ask the app to do (e.g. schedule a live stream, update a title).
  • The app does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • The app does not use your data for ads, profiling or training AI models.
  • The app has no server, no database and no analytics. The developer does not receive your data.

Where data is stored

  • Refresh token: stored on your computer in the operating system's secure vault (Windows Credential Manager).
  • Access tokens: kept only in memory while the app runs.
  • YouTube data: not stored. It is fetched when needed and passed to the AI assistant you connected to the app.
  • Local files (thumbnails, videos, captions): read only from the one folder you allow, and only to upload them to YouTube.

AI assistant

The app passes the results of its actions to the AI assistant (MCP client) that you connect it to. That assistant's own privacy policy applies to data it receives. You choose and control that assistant.

Google API Services User Data Policy

yt-core-mcp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How to remove access and delete data

  • Run yt-core-mcp auth --logout. This revokes the token at Google and deletes it from your computer.
  • Or remove access at any time in your Google account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (also at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • After that the app has no stored data left.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
