yt-core-mcp

yt-core-mcp is a personal tool that lets an AI assistant help manage a YouTube channel.

It is an MCP server: a small program that runs on your own computer and connects an AI assistant (for example Claude) to your YouTube channel through the official YouTube APIs.

New here? Start with the Quick guide. Full details are in the Documentation.

What it does

  • Schedule live streams: title, description, date and time, thumbnail
  • Publish and update live streams
  • Read and update video and channel details
  • Optional: captions, comments and channel analytics (only if you turn them on)

How it works

  • Runs only on your computer. There is no server and no cloud service.
  • You sign in with your Google account in a terminal. The AI assistant cannot sign in for you and never sees your password or tokens.
  • The app asks only for the permissions it needs. Extra permissions are added only when you choose to.
  • Your sign-in token is stored in your computer's secure vault (Windows Credential Manager).
  • Uses only official Google and YouTube APIs.

Your data

yt-core-mcp does not collect, sell or share your data. Read more:

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Service

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google account permissions.
